# 0. Mission

Create the first active Unfict Web Master Composition:

> **A complete Homepage that establishes the visual parent for the rest of the Unfict public website.**

The Homepage must make Unfict understandable, credible, distinctive, and technically serious without becoming a dense platform explanation.

Its purpose is to establish the visual grammar from which later pages — GS1 Digital Link, About, FAQ, and Article — can legitimately evolve.

This is **not** a request to design a component library.

This is **not** a request to expose every Unfict capability.

This is **not** a request to convert the existing Interface Engine placeholders into final visuals.

The task is to design the complete experience first.

# 2. Context

Unfict — Reality Sync — is institutional infrastructure for keeping digital representations aligned with authoritative Reality.

The current public focus is persistent product identity and GS1 Digital Link.

The Homepage serves primarily:

```text
developers
solution architects
digital operations teams
enterprise IT
product-data / connected-product teams
```

The page should feel:

```text
precise
calm
credible
spacious
authoritative
human
technical without being cold
```

# 3. Homepage Job

The Homepage must help a qualified first-time visitor understand:

> **Unfict provides persistent product identity and reliable GS1 Digital Link resolution so product destinations can evolve without forcing teams to operate the resolver stack themselves.**

The visitor should quickly understand:

```text
what problem exists
what Unfict changes
why GS1 Digital Link matters
why this is relevant to developers and solution architects
what the next legitimate action is
```

---

# 4. Primary Communication Hierarchy

The Homepage should communicate in this order:

```text
1. The Job
2. Why persistence matters
3. The conceptual behavior
4. Why developers / teams care
5. How to go deeper

```

# 7. Hero — Visual Job

The Hero is the primary visual identity moment.

It must establish:

```text

GS1 relevance

```

The Hero should combine strong typography with one meaningful visual system.

Preferred visual directions include:

- documentary product / packaging / label imagery;
- a real-world product identity moment;
- a restrained conceptual identity-to-resource visual;
- a composition combining physical product Reality with a simple technical resolution idea.

```text

generic product screenshots
3D chrome objects
abstract gradient blobs
AI imagery
cyber grids
```

It needs to make the promise understandable.

The design may express the relationship approximately as:

```text

→ DIGITAL LINK URI
→ RESOURCE REQUEST
→ RESOLVER
→ RELEVANT RESOURCE
```
