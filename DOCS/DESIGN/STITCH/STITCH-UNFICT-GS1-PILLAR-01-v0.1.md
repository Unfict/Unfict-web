# 19. Problem Region --- Physical Reality Outlives Digital Destinations

**Heading**

> **The product stays. The destination may not.**

**Body**

> A physical label can outlive a campaign, a website path, a service
> provider, a market page, or a documentation URL.

> The physical carrier should not have to carry the lifecycle of every
> digital destination.

> The useful design problem is to keep the product's standards-based
> identity dependable while the resources around it can change.

This region should feel recognizable and concrete.

Do not turn it into a grid of enterprise pain points.

# 20. GS1 Digital Link --- Plain-Language Explanation

**Heading**

> **GS1 Digital Link gives product identity a web-native form.**

**Body**

> A QR code is a carrier. GS1 Digital Link defines how GS1 identifiers
> and related data can be expressed in a form the Web can resolve.

> That creates a useful separation: the physical product can carry a
> standards-based identity while the digital resource behind the
> interaction can be managed independently.

Do not imply that every QR code is GS1 Digital Link.

Do not imply that GS1 Digital Link itself is an Unfict invention.

# 26. Short FAQ

Keep the initial pillar FAQ short.

Three questions are enough.

## Question 1

> **Is GS1 Digital Link just a QR code?**

**Answer**

> No. A QR code is a data carrier. GS1 Digital Link defines how GS1
> identifiers and related data can be expressed in a web-resolvable
> form. The carrier and the identity / linking semantics are different
> layers.

## Question 2

> **Do I have to change the physical product every time a destination
> changes?**

**Answer**

> A central reason to use persistent resolution is to avoid binding the
> physical identity permanently to one changeable web destination. The
> exact implementation depends on how the identity and resources are
> configured.

## Question 3

> **Is Unfict a GS1 standards body?**

**Answer**

> No. Unfict is not GS1 and does not define GS1 standards. Unfict is
> building infrastructure intended to interoperate with applicable GS1
> standards.

Do not add a large FAQ merely because a reusable FAQ pattern exists.
