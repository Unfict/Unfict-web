CLARIFICATIONS

Frequently asked questions

What does Unfict actually do? expand_more

In plain terms: we keep physical product codes connected to active digital destinations as websites and systems change. When someone scans a barcode or QR code on a box years from now, Unfict ensures it resolves to the right authoritative place rather than a broken link.

Why can't our packaging QR code just point directly to our website? expand_more

Because websites inevitably change. Redesigns, new domains, reorganized CMS paths, and expired marketing promotions will break direct links. Once a physical product leaves the factory, its printed code cannot be edited. Pointing directly to an ephemeral webpage creates permanent link rot.

What happens when our product information or marketing changes? expand_more

You update the destination rule in Unfict. The barcode on the physical item remains unchanged, but incoming scans immediately route to the new campaign, the revised user manual, or the updated digital product passport without requiring any physical reprinting.

Where does GS1 Digital Link fit into this? expand_more

GS1 Digital Link provides the international open standard for encoding product identifiers (like GTIN barcodes) into web URIs. Unfict provides the high-availability infrastructure that resolves those standardized requests reliably and contextually.

Is Unfict for business teams or developers? expand_more

Both. Business and product teams gain durable customer engagement and regulatory continuity across decades of product lifecycles. Engineering teams get reliable, zero-maintenance resolution infrastructure that handles standards compliance, edge latency, and failover out of the box.
