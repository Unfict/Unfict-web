01 / THE LIFECYCLE MISMATCH

The product stays.
The destination may not.

A physical label can outlive a campaign, a website path, a service provider, a market page, or a documentation URL.

The physical carrier should not have to carry the lifecycle of every digital destination. The useful design problem is to keep the product's standards-based identity dependable while the resources around it can change.

PHYSICAL PERMANENCE Packaging Lifespan
5 to 20+ years in circulation, global supply chains, secondary markets, and end-user hands. STABLE
DIGITAL VOLATILITY Destination Lifespan
12 to 36 months before CMS migration, corporate rebrand, localized link rewrites, or domain restructuring. VOLATILE
RESOLUTION LAYER Continuous Alignment
The physical barcode remains completely untouched; resolver maps standard identifiers to live authorized endpoints. RESOLVED
02 / STANDARDS SEPARATION

GS1 Digital Link gives product identity a web-native form.

A QR code is a carrier. GS1 Digital Link defines how GS1 identifiers and related data can be expressed in a form the Web can resolve. That creates a useful separation: the physical product can carry a standards-based identity while the digital resource behind the interaction can be managed independently.

04 / OPERATIONAL ROLE & BOUNDARIES

Keep the GS1 Job clear without making resolver operations the product your team has to own.

Teams can build and operate resolver capability themselves. Unfict is being built as standards-native resolver infrastructure so teams can work with the GS1 Job without turning ordinary resolver operation into another permanent internal product to maintain.

Unfict is not the destination. It should connect to the systems that already own the relevant content and operational state.

BOUNDARY 01

Not a Standards Body

Unfict is not GS1 and does not define GS1 standards. We engineer high-availability infrastructure strictly compliant with published global specs.

BOUNDARY 02

Not a CMS or Builder

Unfict is not a landing-page builder, headless CMS, or marketing microsite hosting tool. Content authoring lives in dedicated publishing systems.

BOUNDARY 03

Not an ERP or PIM

Unfict is not intended to replace ERP, PIM, PLM, or other systems that already own master product records or operational lifecycle state.

LONG-TERM STANDARDS MAINTENANCE

Standards support does not end when the first link works. Real implementations need to keep up with identifier forms, resource behavior, resolver behavior, and evolving standards guidance.

06 / FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Clear standards clarity.

Essential architectural answers on separation of concerns between physical carriers, identity standards, and digital link resolution.

Is GS1 Digital Link just a QR code? 01

No. A QR code is a data carrier. GS1 Digital Link defines how GS1 identifiers and related data can be expressed in a web-resolvable form. The carrier and the identity / linking semantics are different layers.

Do I have to change the physical product every time a destination changes? 02

A central reason to use persistent resolution is to avoid binding the physical identity permanently to one changeable web destination. The exact implementation depends on how the identity and resources are configured.

Is Unfict a GS1 standards body? 03

No. Unfict is not GS1 and does not define GS1 standards. Unfict is building infrastructure intended to interoperate with applicable GS1 standards.
